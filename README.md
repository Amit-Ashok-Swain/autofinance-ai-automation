## 🏎️ Executive Overview

**AutoFinance AI™** is an end-to-end financial operations and intelligent automation suite purpose-built for multi-location automotive dealership networks (Passenger & Commercial Vehicles, Authorized Service Workshops, Regional Spares Depots, and Satellite Showrooms).

The platform transforms manual, error-prone dealership accounting into an automated, real-time command center:
- **Vehicle Sales & Margin Accounting:** Real-time computation of Ex-showroom yield, RTO taxes, Motor Insurance commissions, Mandatory accessories margin, Extended warranty, and Section 206C(1F) TCS.
- **Financier & DO Subvention Reconciliation:** Automated tracking of loan sanctions vs bank credits, loan payout commissions (1.5% - 3.2%), OEM subventions, and aging recovery.
- **Workshop & Service Revenue Automation:** Job-card lifecycle management, technician billability & productivity scoring, spare parts gross margin (28% GST), and cashless insurance claim deductions.
- **OEM Claims & Incentive Cross-Matcher:** Automated cross-matching of quarterly volume target rebates, warranty parts reimbursements, and co-op marketing fund credit notes.
- **Statutory GST 3-Way ITC Matcher:** Inward Purchase Register vs GSTR-2B automated reconciliation with Section 17(5) blocked credit filters and automated vendor discrepancy notices.
- **Anti-Fraud Maker-Checker Security:** 4-step dual-authorization checklist releasing digital QR gate-passes only upon full payment and confirmed bank DO.
- **13-Week Cash Forecasting:** Dynamic Working Capital & Cash Conversion Cycle (`CCC = DIO + DSO - DPO`) simulator.
- **Autonomous AI Copilot:** Instant natural language query resolver trained on automotive accounting and GST compliance.
